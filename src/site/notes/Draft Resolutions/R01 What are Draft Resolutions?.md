The end goal of most councils is to pass a **resolution**, a document that lists the solutions to tackle the debated issues that the council supports.

![Screenshot 2026-05-15 at 9.31.40 PM.png](/img/user/00%20Misc/Screenshot%202026-05-15%20at%209.31.40%20PM.png)

Before a resolution is a resolution, it is known as a **draft resolution**. Draft resolutions are voted upon by the council before becoming a resolution.

Because different delegates have different intentions and goals, there are often multiple draft resolutions to be voted on.
