Some MUNs have a council called Press Corps (or International Press Gallery) where delegates write articles representing news agencies. These delegates spectate council proceedings.

Usually, every council will have a **press conference** with press delegates, where press delegates are given the opportunity to ask questions to specific delegates, groups of delegates, or the entire council.

Press conferences are held by the **editors** of the Press Corps (a.k.a. the Dais of the Press Corps).

Delegates’ responses to the questions during the press conference can affect your performance and therefore your potential for awards.
