This guide is just a start to your MUN journey! There are many exceptions to what I’ve written here, and it’s very possible that there are many mistakes because I am simply one (1) person.

If you have any questions or suggestions to update this guide, feel free to message me on Instagram or Telegram **@onedollopofmayo**!

# Why I wrote this guide

I got bored and signed up for SMUN 2025 and YMAX 2025 with my friends. My friends aren’t MUNners, so I figured I’d write something for them.

I got carried away and started spending hours working on this guide instead of preparing for both the MUNs. Actually, I started to use this guide as an excuse not to start preparing for it. Oops.

Sure hope this helps!
